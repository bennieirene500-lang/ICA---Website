Lesson 1

AI Operating System (Part 1)

This lesson shows you how to stop treating ChatGPT like a blank tool and start building an AI system that understands who you are, who you help and how your business works.

Download the resources below and work through them while watching the lesson. Do not only watch the training. Build your own operating system as you go.

What you will build

  • A clear description of who you are and what you stand for
  • A definition of the person your business helps
  • Your business and marketing principles
  • Instructions for how ChatGPT should think
  • Your preferred writing and communication style
  • A reusable AI Operating System
Important

Do not rush this training

Your answers become the foundation of your AI system. Clear and honest answers will produce stronger results than impressive-sounding answers.
